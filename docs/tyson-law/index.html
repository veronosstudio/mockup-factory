Do you handle common-law separations, or only divorce?

Both. Common-law relationships have their own rules, especially around property division and guardianship of children, and Brent keeps up with how those rules change.

My child has been apprehended. Can Brent help?

Yes. Brent helps parents understand the child welfare process and what needs to happen for their children to be returned to their care, while working toward the best possible outcome for parents and children. Call the office as soon as you can.

Does my divorce have to go to court?

Not always. Issues can be resolved by a negotiated settlement or through court proceedings. Brent can advise you on both, and when court is needed, his office is right across the street.

Can you keep my original will safe?

Yes. Brent prepares affordable wills and can store the original for you if you'd rather not keep it yourself.

Who do I contact about buying or selling my home?

Email Debbie at debbie@tysonlaw.ca. She handles real estate conveyancing for the office.
