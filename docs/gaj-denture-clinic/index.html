Meet your denturist

Jamie Swiscoski, Licensed Denturist & Owner

Jamie has owned GAJ Denture Clinic since 2010. She graduated from NAIT's Denturist Technology program in 2002, worked seven years in a Brandon clinic, and has been a licensed denturist since 2006.

Patients are always her first priority. If something new isn't right for you, she will be the first to say so. We never push treatments or products.

  • NAIT Denturist Technology diploma
